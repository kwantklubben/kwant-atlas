### Ch 18 — Calibration to market data  (pp 669–672)
Verified content:
- **Purpose:** calibrate the JY model and the two inflation market models (first market model "MM1", second market model "MM2") to Euro YYIIS (year-on-year inflation-indexed swap) fixed rates as of **October 7, 2004**; some params pre-fitted to at-the-money nominal cap volatilities.
- **Parameter reduction:** JY formula (16.17) has 7 params (`a_n, σ_n, a_r, σ_r, σ_I, ρ_{n,r}, ρ_{r,I}`) reduced to **5** by fitting `a_n, σ_n` to ATM nominal caps first. MM1 (16.27): 5 params/yr → global `c1..c5`; each `σ_{n,i}` auto-calibrated to ATM caps; `σ_{r,i}=c1/[1+c2(T_i−T_2)]`, `σ_{I,1}=c3`, `ρ_i=c4`, `ρ_{I,r,i}=c5`. MM2 (16.32): 5 → **4** params `c1..c4`; `ρ_{I,i}=1−(1−c1)exp(−c2T_{i−1})`, `ρ_{I,n,i}=c3`, `σ_{I,i}=c4`. M=20 yearly payments.
- **Results:** Table 18.1 — max |model−market| YYIIS-rate gap is **0.7 bp**, negligible vs typical 5–10 bp bid/ask. The three models fit YYIIS equally well but **differ materially away-from-the-money** (tested via zero-strike floors, Fig. 18.1, maturities 4–20y; JY vs MM2). Recommendation: do **not** calibrate to swaps alone — include option (zero-strike floor) prices (Fig. 18.2 joint fit).
- **Remark 18.0.1:** constant CPI volatility ⇒ implied **terminal correlation among forward inflation rates too low** regardless of instantaneous `ρ_{I,i}`; suggests time-dependent `σ_{I,i}`.
- **Remark 18.0.2 (smile):** flat-smile market models adequate for many purposes; smile handled either by uncertain-vol extension (Gatarek 2003; Brigo–Mercurio–Rapisarda 2004, cf. Ch 12) or by Mercurio–Moreni (2006) stochastic-vol framework of Ch 19.

### Ch 19 — Introducing Stochastic Volatility  (pp 673–689)
Verified content (Mercurio & Moreni 2006, Heston-style):
- **Motivation:** ATM-implied vol of II caplets underestimates OTM and overestimates ITM (skew; USD data Nov 3, 2004, Fig. 19.1) ⇒ need smile-capable model.
- **Model (19.2):** nominal forward LIBORs lognormal LFM (constant vols); forward CPIs `I_i` carry common mean-reverting square-root vol `V`:`dF_i/F_i = σ_i^F dZ^{Q,F}`, `dI_i/I_i = σ_i^I √V dZ^{Q,I}`, `dV = α(θ−V)dt + ε√V dW`, positivity `2αθ > ε²`. Volatility dynamics specified under the **spot-LIBOR measure Q_d** (payoff-independent; avoids terminal-measure dependence and market-price-of-volatility issue).
- **Under forward measure Q_j** the SDEs for `I_j, I_{j−1}, X_j = ln(I_j/I_{j−1})` and V have drift terms in `F_l` (19.4–19.5, with `m_j(t)`).
- **Pricing:** II caplet (19.6) = P(t,T_j)·∫(e^s−e^k)^+ q_j(s)ds, computed by **Carr–Madan Fourier transform** of the conditional characteristic function `φ_jt(u)=E^{j}_t[e^{iu X_j(T_j)}]` (19.7; floorlet in App. B with η̃ damping + put-call parity 19.36).
- **Uncorrelated case (19.9 ρ^{F,I}=0, 19.10 ρ^{F,V}=0):** explicit φ via iterated Heston PDEs — `E^{j}_{T_{j−1}}[e^{iuY_j(T_j)}] = exp{A_Y + B_Y V(T_{j−1}) + iuY_j(T_{j−1})}` with Riccati-type A_Y/B_Y (19.13) and composite A_X/B_X (19.15–19.16). Keeps `ρ^{I}_{i,l}` and `ρ^{I,V}_i` nonzero.
- **Approximations for nonzero correlations:** (a) freeze drift terms at 0 and shift vol asymptote `θ̃ = θ − Σ D_l(0)τ_l σ_l^F ρ^{F,V}_l /α` (19.18); (b) produce linear-in-V drift via `ᾱ, θ̄` (19.21–19.22). Both keep φ in closed form; Remark 19.2.1 positivity `2θ̃α>ε²`, `2θ̄ᾱ>ε²`. Caplet price differences between the two approximations (and vs exact) < 0.2% (EUR Oct 7 2004 test).
- **Calibration (19.3):** USD caplet prices stripped from caps (Tables 19.1/19.2), `I_i(0)=I(0)(1+S(T_i))^i`, I(0)=190.91 (Nov 3, 2004), correlations parameterized `ρ^{I}_{i,i−1}=1−(1−ρ_0)e^{−λT_{i−2}}` (19.23); scale-invariance normalization `σ_1^I=1`; 2M−5 free params; good short-maturity fit. Appendices: A = Heston PDE characteristic-function solution; B = floorlet FT pricing + put-call parity.
- **Errors/gaps in existing extraction:** Ch 19 is **absent as a chapter** (merged into "15–20 Inflation" bullet, which merely says "stochastic-volatility inflation"). Not captured before: Mercurio–Moreni attribution + spot-LIBOR measure choice, the forward-CPI ratio payoff & X_j dynamics, Heston-like `φ_jt` via two iterated PDEs, the two freezing approximations (θ̃ and ᾱθ̄), Carr–Madan caplet/floorlet formulas, the 2M−5 calibration scheme, parameter numbers and test dates.

### Ch 22 — Intensity Models  (pp 757–841)
Verified content:
- **22.2 Poisson/Cox:** time-homogeneous Poisson (constant intensity; `Q{τ̄>t}=e^{−γ̄t}`; exponential inter-arrival; intensity = default prob per unit time), time-inhomogeneous (γ(t), `Γ(t)=∫_0^t γ`, `τ=Γ^{-1}(ξ)`, ξ~Exp(1)); Cox / doubly-stochastic processes (stochastic λ, `Λ(τ)=ξ`, `Q{τ>t}=E[e^{−Λ(t)}]`). Core analogy: **survival probability = discount factor, intensity = instantaneous credit spread**; reduced-form models incomplete (external ξ).
- **22.3 CDS calibration / implied hazard:** piecewise-linear & piecewise-constant γ stripping; **Parmalat case study** (CDS data 2003: Sep 10 [REC 40%], Nov 28, Dec 8 [REC 25%], Dec 10 [REC 15%]); distressed-date pathologies (negative intensities with PR/PR2 postponed payoffs; piecewise-constant more robust than piecewise-linear); comparison note vs structural AT1P calibration (Brigo–Tarenghi, Morini).
- **22.4 dependence:** interest rates & default independent under deterministic intensity; **only route to r–default dependence is stochastic intensity correlated with r**; `P̄(0,T)=P(0,T)Q(τ>T)` under independence, else `E[e^{−∫(r+λ)}]`.
- **22.5 filtration switching formula (22.3):** `E(1_{τ>T}X|G_t) = (1_{τ>t}/Q(τ>t|F_t))E(1_{τ>T}X|F_t)`; proof given; enables CDS market model under a measure equivalent to risk-neutral.
- **22.6 simulation:** `τ=Λ^{-1}(ξ)`; threshold-barrier `B̄` conditional simulation (`ξ|ξ<B̄`, efficiency gain ~×7–13); standard error / confidence windows (2.33·Std/√m for 98%); control-variate variance reduction with `k*=−Corr·Std/Std`; natural control `Π^{an}=1_{τ<T}` etc.
- **22.7 SSRD (Brigo–Alfonsi):** Shifted Square-Root Diffusion = **CIR++ for r** (`r_t=x^α_t+ϕ`, dx=k(θ−x)dt+σ√x dW, ϕ=f^{mkt}−f^{CIR}) **correlated (ρ) with CIR++ for λ** (`λ_t=y^β_t+ψ`, dy=κ(µ−y)dt+ν√y dZ, `2κµ>ν²`). Separable calibration at ρ=0: `ψ(t,β)=Γ^{mkt}(t)+ln P^{CIR}(0,t,y_0;β)` (22.12); CDS value then matches model-independent formula (22.9). ρ≠0 ⇒ joint solve (22.14), but **numerically ρ has negligible impact on plain CDS prices** (Tables 22.21/22.22). Discretization schemes preserving positivity: Euler, Milstein, modified (Deelstra–Delbaen, Diop reflected), **implicit (Brigo–Alfonsi 22.18) and implicit-on-square-root (Alfonsi 22.19)**, explicit family E(λ) (22.20); strong/weak convergence study + Lemma 22.7.1; preferred E(0). **Gaussian dependence mapping** (22.7.5): replace correlated (x,y) CIR by correlated Vasicek (`x^{V},y^{V}`) matching bond-expectation (Lemmas 22.7.2/22.7.3, approx 22.26/22.27), enabling closed-form ρ≠0 CDS/functional terms; MC-validated. Correlation impact on test payoffs A–H & a **cancellable swap** example (correlation can matter for such nonlinear payoffs, ~15 bp under stress). **CDS options & Jamshidian decomposition (22.7.9):** closed form for payer CDS option under deterministic r + CIR++ λ (reduction to ZCB-put-type terms at critical `y*`, eq 22.34–22.35); full SSRD via MC (sub-path method + error bound); implied-vol patterns (Table 22.28: σ_{a,b}imp ↓κ, ↑µ,↑ν,↑y0, ↓ρ). 22.7.10 Bermudan CDS options (Ben Ameur–Brigo–Errais, dynamic programming with finite elements).
- **22.8 JCIR/JCIR++:** diffusion intensity insufficient (SSRD caps implied vol ~30% vs market >50%; positivity constraint `2κµ>ν²` forces large κ that counteracts ν). Jump-extension: `dλ=κ(µ−λ)dt+ν√λ dZ+dJ^{α,γ}`, J compound-Poisson with exp(γ) jumps (affine jump diffusion, positive jumps keep λ>0). Survival `Q{τ>T|G_t}=1_{τ>t}ᾱ(t,T)e^{−β̄(t,T)λ_t}=P^{JCIR}` (recast a la CIR bond formula; special-case limit at h=κ+2γ). **JCIR++ shift** `Ψ^J(t,β)=Γ^{mkt}(t)+ln P^{JCIR}(0,t,λ_0;β)` (22.40); 6 free params (κ,µ,ν,λ0,α,γ). Simulation via Mikulevicius–Platen; Jamshidian decomposition + **extended transform** (`ψ(z,T_a,λ_0)=E[e^{zλ_{T_a}−∫λ}]`, eqs 22.44–22.49) → semi-explicit Π1/Π2; attains high implied vol (σ^{imp}=28% no jumps, 40% (α=γ=0.1), 57% (α=γ=0.15), CDS rates still realistic). Multi-name possibility: correlate jumps across names keeping ξ independent.

### Ch 23 — CDS Options Market Models  (pp 841–874)
Verified content:
- **Setup:** modelling `R_{a,b}` directly ("model dR without dλ", parallel to dS without dr). **DPVBP / defaultable-annuity numeraire** `Ĉ_{a,b}(t)=Q(τ>t|F_t)C̄_{a,b}(t)`, `C̄_{a,b}(t)=Σ_{i=a+1}^b α_i P̄(t,T_i)`. By dropping the survival indicator the numeraire stays strictly positive ⇒ measure `Q̂_{a,b}` **equivalent to risk-neutral** (contrast with Schönbucher's survival measure, which is not equivalent); follows Jamshidian (2004)/Brigo (2004,2005)/Brigo–Morini (2005).
- **CDS option payoff:** `1_{τ>T_a}D(t,T_a)Σα_iP̄(T_a,T_i)(R_{a,b}(T_a)−K)⁺` (payer). \(R_{a,b}\) = (upfront-CDS price)/(Ĉ) is a Q̂-martingale; assume lognormal `dR_{a,b}=σ_{a,b}R_{a,b}dŴ^{a,b}` ⇒ **Black market formula (23.13)** `Call=1_{τ>t}C̄_{a,b}(t)[R_{a,b}(t)N(d1)−K N(d2)]`, `d_{1,2}=[ln(R/K)±½σ²(T_a−t)]/(σ√(T_a−t))`. Used as an **implied-vol quoting mechanism**. PR (exact) and PR2 (`Č_{a,b}(t)=Σα_i E[D(t,T_i)1_{τ>T_{i−1}}]`, eq 23.14/23.15) variants; DFRN1/DFRN2 market formulas; once-callable DFRN.
- **Implied volatilities from market (23.2.3):** Euro names C1=Deutsche Telekom, C2=DaimlerChrysler, C3=France Telecom, quotes **March 26, 2004, REC=0.4**: mid option prices, CDS forward rates, σ^{imp} ≈ **50.2–54.7%** — high vs swaptions but consistent with Hull–White (2003) historical estimates; implied vols robust to recovery (Table 23.2) and to shifts of the ZC curve (Table 23.3); CDS spot-rate/curve inputs (Tables 23.4/23.5).
- **Vector CDS market model (23.3)** — three constructions for the family of CDS forward rates under one measure (built on PR rates): (1) **one- and two-period rates** (R_k, R_{k−2,k}), closure via 21.28/23.16–23.17 and full change-of-numeraire drift derivation (vector SDE 23.19, Milstein); (2) **co-terminal + one-period rates** (23.20); (3) **approximated one-period rates** `R̃_j` (23.21, freezing F_j at 0) giving a closed drift term `dR̃_i = R̃_i(µ̃ dt + σ_i dZ_i)` (23.22) usable for MC/CMCDS.
- **Smile (23.4):** local-vol / CEV / displaced-diffusion / mixture dynamics replacing (23.12)/(23.14) (recycle Ch 9–12 LIBOR smile dynamics).
- **CMCDS (23.5):** Prop 23.5.1 (Brigo 2005b) approximated price (23.23) as weighted sum over `Σα_jP̄(0,T_j)` of `R̃_i(0)·exp{T_{j−1}σ_i·Σ_{k=j+1}^i ρ_{j,k}σ_k R̃_k(0)/(R̃_k(0)+LGD/α_k)} − R_j(0)`; the exponential term = **convexity adjustment**; at ρ=0 reduces to `Σα_jP̄(0,T_j)(R_{j−1,j+c}(0)−R_{j−1,j}(0))` (23.24). One-period rate stripping from spot CDS rates; **FIAT numerical example** (Dec 20, 2004, REC=0.4; convexity and participation-rate tables; convexity moves 5y-5y6m participation rate ~71.6%→66.8% at σ=0.4, ρ=0.9).
