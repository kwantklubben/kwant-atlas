# Track: Business & Corporate Finance

Welcome! If you study Business Administration, Finance, Economics, or Accounting, you hold a vital intuition that pure mathematicians and software developers often lack:
- You understand **why companies make money**, how management guidance impacts valuation, and how macro cycles shift industry profit margins.
- You can read financial statements, cash flow statements, and earnings conference calls.

The challenge is converting qualitative economic hypotheses into **testable, bias-free, programmatic strategies**.

## Recommended Starting Nodes
1. **[[pillars/02-stat-arb-trading/fundamental-multi-factor-models|Fundamental Multi-Factor Models]]**: Learn how Fama-French and Barra quantify Value, Quality, and Size into mathematical factors.
2. **[[pillars/06-machine-learning-quant/financial-nlp-and-transcripts|Financial NLP & Transcripts (Quartr)]]**: Mine earnings call transcripts and 10-K filings to model Post-Earnings Announcement Drift (PEAD).
3. **[[pillars/04-portfolio-risk/markowitz-covariance-shrinkage|Markowitz & Covariance Shrinkage]]**: Understand how modern portfolio construction balances risk and return across diversified holdings.

## The Python Bridge
- In the KwantKlubben workshop, you don't need to build low-level matching engines.
- You only need to write a `target_weights(data)` function in Pandas that maps economic signals into portfolio allocations.
- Pair up with a CS or Physics member via the club's cross-disciplinary pairing to combine domain insights with fast code!
